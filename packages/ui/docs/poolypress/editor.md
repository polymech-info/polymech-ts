# AI-Assisted Content Creation

PoolyPress integrates powerful AI aids directly into your publishing workspace, fundamentally augmenting how you create and assemble content.

- **Interactive Chat Panel:** Converse directly with your content using the built-in Chat interface. You can ask the AI to draft text, attach media, or even directly drop in interactive gallery widgets. The panel is fully context-aware and hooks directly into the site's own internal search engine, allowing the AI to instantly find, query, and link your existing posts, pages, and categories.
- **AI Layout Wizard:** Say goodbye to starting from a blank page. Describe what you want—like "a product page with a hero image, a 2-column features grid, and a reviews section"—and the AI Layout Wizard autonomously generates the exact widget containers needed. It reads your page's live template variables to produce highly realistic, data-driven placeholder content.
- **Contextual Inline Tools:** Within the editor itself, intelligent markdown tools let you rewrite, expand, summarize, and translate your prose on the fly—ensuring your copy is always beautifully tailored for your target audiences without ever leaving your workflow.
