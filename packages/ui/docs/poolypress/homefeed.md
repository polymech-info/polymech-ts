# Editable Home & User Profiles

PolyMech's home and user pages are fully customizable, avoiding rigid templates in favor of a dynamic, extensible widget system. 

- **Versatile Layouts:** Seamlessly toggle between robust grid, list, and large gallery views to best showcase your feeds.
- **Extensible Widgets:** Effortlessly drag and drop interactive elements—from media sliders to text content—into your pages, powered by an integrated widget registry.
- **Mobile-First & Responsive:** Every layout is highly responsive, guaranteeing that your feeds and pages deliver a flawless viewing experience across all devices.
